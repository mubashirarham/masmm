Knowledge Base & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers to common questions about our services, payments, speed, and safety.

What is MAsmmpanel and how does an SMM panel work?
MAsmmpanel is an automated Social Media Marketing (SMM) reseller platform. We provide an intuitive web interface and high-speed API that connects creators and agencies with servers delivering followers, likes, views, watch time, and comments for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and Telegram. All orders are processed in real time with wholesale PKR pricing.
How do I deposit money using EasyPaisa or JazzCash?
Log into your dashboard, click "Add Funds", and choose your preferred method:
  • Direct EasyPaisa & JazzCash: Instant PKR deposit via mobile wallet.
  • Bank Transfer & Sadapay / Nayapay: Send funds to the verified account details provided and submit your Transaction ID (TID) for immediate approval.
  • Cryptocurrency: Instant automated credit with Binance Pay and USDT.
How fast are orders processed and delivered?
The vast majority of our 480+ services start within 0 to 60 seconds. Full completion time depends on the quantity and server speed, with large orders delivered smoothly in natural batches to protect your account's algorithmic health.
Is it safe for my social media accounts? Do you need my password?
100% Safe. We will NEVER ask for your password or private login credentials. All we need is the public link to your profile or post. Always ensure your account privacy is set to Public before placing an order.
What happens if my followers or likes drop?
Services marked with the Refill badge are covered by our automatic refill guarantee (typically 30 to 365 days). If any drop occurs, simply go to your "My Orders" tab and click "Refill" to automatically replenish the count for free.
Can I start my own SMM panel business and resell your services?
Yes! We are a primary provider for hundreds of Pakistani resellers. You can connect your website script (RentalPanel, SmartPanel, etc.) to our API at https://digitalmarketplace.store/api/v2 using your API Key and resell with your own customized profit margins.
